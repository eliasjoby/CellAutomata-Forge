About Conway's Game of Life

The Game of Life is a zero-player simulation where every generation is calculated from the previous one. Each cell is either alive or dead and changes state using only its 8 neighbors.

  1. Any live cell with fewer than two live neighbors dies.
  2. Any live cell with two or three live neighbors survives.
  3. Any live cell with more than three live neighbors dies.
  4. Any dead cell with exactly three live neighbors becomes alive.
